Cake Cups by BreadWorks: A Complete Case Study

The Challenge

Cake Cups—handcrafted desserts made daily in Greencastle, Indiana—had strong in-store sales ($182K/year) but virtually zero online presence ($2,845 in revenue over two years). My team was tasked with increasing online sales. What began as a website redesign became a complete rebranding campaign.

What We Discovered

After analyzing the data, we realized the problem wasn't the product (70% repeat customer rate) or the website mechanics. The problem was visibility and identity. Cake Cups had no consistent brand presence, no professional marketing assets, and no way for new customers to even find the product online.

Instead of optimizing the wrong things, we proposed a complete rebranding campaign: professional photoshoots, website redesign, social media strategy, and paid advertising.
